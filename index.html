Everything Gusk does

The complete feature list.

Every action that changes your mail runs only when you ask for it. Trashed mail goes to your mailbox’s Trash, where you can restore it; Gusk deletes mail permanently only when you choose to empty Spam or Trash.

Read & organise

  • Connects with an app password to Gmail, iCloud Mail, Yahoo Mail, Zoho Mail or any IMAP service, and shows your folders and labels
  • Several accounts side by side in one unified inbox
  • Lists your mail and groups it by sender, domain and type (newsletters, promotions, notifications, receipts)
  • Opens full messages, threads and attachments
  • Searches your mailbox
  • Creates, renames and deletes labels or folders, and files messages into them
  • Shows sender logos, looked up by domain name only

Clean up

  • Sweep: archive, trash, mark read or unread, star or label all mail from a sender or domain at once
  • Suggests cleanups (for example old promotions) and asks you to confirm each one
  • Unsubscribe: uses the sender’s own unsubscribe link, either opening it in your browser or sending the unsubscribe email the sender asked for
  • Finds large emails and emails with attachments so you can free up space
  • Remembers your choice for a sender, only when you ask, so their future mail is archived or trashed automatically on your device
  • Empties Spam or Trash, only when you ask
  • Undo for sweeps and bulk actions

Write

  • Compose, reply and forward, with rich text and attachments
  • Saves and discards drafts in your mailbox
  • Email signatures, stored as one small “Winnow Settings” message in your own mailbox so they sync across your devices
  • Voice dictation, processed on your device

On-device intelligence

  • Ask Gusk: ask questions about your mail and get answers and summaries
  • Ask Gusk can suggest actions (archive, label, unsubscribe) and always asks you to confirm first
  • Next 7 days: bookings, bills, deliveries and to-dos found in your inbox
  • All of this runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. No email is sent to any AI service or used to train AI models.

Your accounts

Signs in with an app password.

Gusk connects directly from your device to your mail service’s own servers. It has no server, and the developer never receives your data.

IMAP over TLS

Used to read your messages so they can be listed, grouped and opened, and to archive, trash, label, star and mark them read or unread when you ask.

SMTP over TLS

Used only to send email you write, and an unsubscribe email when you tap Unsubscribe and the sender asks for one.

An app password is a separate password your mail service creates for one app; your main password is never used. You can revoke it at any time in your mail service’s account settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Private by design

Your email never leaves your hands.

Gusk AI has no server. Your mail goes straight from your mail service to your device, and the AI features run on the device with Apple Intelligence. Read the privacy policy →

  • No servers, no copies of your mail
  • AI runs on your device, never in a third-party cloud
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking
  • App passwords kept in your device’s Keychain
