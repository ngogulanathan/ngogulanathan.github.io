Everything Gusk does

The complete feature list.

Every action that changes your mail runs only when you ask for it. Gusk never deletes mail permanently: trashed mail stays in Gmail’s Trash, where you can restore it for 30 days.

Read & organise

  • Signs in with your Google account and shows your Gmail folders and labels
  • Lists your mail and groups it by sender, domain and type (newsletters, promotions, notifications, receipts)
  • Opens full messages, threads and attachments
  • Searches your mailbox
  • Creates, renames and deletes labels, and applies labels to messages
  • Shows sender logos, looked up by domain name only

Clean up

  • Sweep: archive, trash, mark read or unread, star or label all mail from a sender or domain at once
  • Suggests cleanups (for example old promotions) and asks you to confirm each one
  • Unsubscribe: uses the sender’s own unsubscribe link, either opening it in your browser or sending the unsubscribe email the sender asked for
  • Finds large emails and emails with attachments so you can free up space
  • Creates Gmail filters, only when you ask, so a sender’s future mail is archived or trashed automatically
  • Undo for sweeps and bulk actions

Write

  • Compose, reply and forward, with rich text and attachments
  • Saves and discards drafts in your Gmail
  • Email signatures, stored as one small “Winnow Settings” message in your own Gmail so they sync across your devices
  • Voice dictation, processed on your device

On-device intelligence

  • Ask Gusk: ask questions about your mail and get answers and summaries
  • Ask Gusk can suggest actions (archive, label, unsubscribe) and always asks you to confirm first
  • Next 7 days: bookings, bills, deliveries and to-dos found in your inbox
  • All of this runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. No email is sent to any AI service or used to train AI models.

Google permissions

Gusk asks for only two permissions.

Gusk talks to Gmail directly from your device. It has no server, and the developer never receives your data.

gmail.modify

Needed to read your messages so they can be listed, grouped and opened, and to archive, trash, label, star and mark them read or unread when you ask. Also used to send email and save drafts that you write.

gmail.settings.basic

Used only to create a Gmail filter when you choose to handle a sender’s future mail automatically.

Gusk’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the privacy policy for details.
