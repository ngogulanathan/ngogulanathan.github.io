Gusk AI

Keep what matters. Sweep the rest.

Gusk AI is a Gmail app for Mac, iPhone and iPad. It groups the newsletters, promotions and notifications that bury your real mail, and lets you clear whole senders with a swipe — while the email that matters stays right where it is.

What it does

  • Sweep — archive or trash a whole sender or domain in one swipe, with Undo.
  • Ask Gusk — ask about your mail (“what needs a reply?”, “catch me up on this week”) and have it act for you, with a confirmation first.
  • Next 7 days — bookings, bills, packages and to-dos, pulled out of your inbox.
  • Write — rich-text replies and signatures that follow you across your devices.

Private by design

Gusk AI has no server. Your mail goes directly from Google to your device, and the AI features run on your device using Apple Intelligence. Your email is never sent to us or to any AI service. Read the privacy policy.
