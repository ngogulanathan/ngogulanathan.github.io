How Gusk AI works

Gusk AI reads your email, groups it so it's easy to clean up, and changes it only when you ask. Gusk AI has no server: your mail goes between your device and your own mail service. Every cleanup action can be undone.

2. Signing in

flowchart TD
    A([Open Gusk AI]) --> B{Is an account
signed in?}
    B -- Yes --> Z([Your inbox])
    B -- No --> C[Sign-in screen]
    C --> D[Pick your mail service
Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, Zoho, IMAP]
    D --> E[Enter your email address
and an app password]
    E --> F{Accepted by your
mail service?}
    F -- No --> E
    F -- Yes --> H[App password saved in the
Keychain on this device]
    H --> Z
  • An app password is a separate password your mail service creates for one app. Your main password is never used, and you can revoke the app password at any time.
  • Gusk AI has no accounts of its own, so there's nothing to create. Signing in connects the app directly to your mail service.

3. Getting your mail

flowchart TD
    A[Connect to your mail service
IMAP over TLS, encrypted] --> B[Download your emails]
    B --> C[(Stored on this device
protected by iOS / macOS data protection)]
    C --> D[Inbox, Browse and Sweep update]
    C --> E{New mail or
a sync problem?}
    E -- Yes --> F[Notification and badge]
  • Your mail goes directly between this device and your mail service. It's never copied to a Gusk AI server, because there isn't one.
  • Email you write is sent through your mail service over SMTP with TLS.

4. Bundles

flowchart TD
    A[An email] --> B{Sender on a shared domain
like gmail.com?}
    B -- No --> C[Bundled by company domain
all mail from shop.example]
    B -- Yes --> D[Bundled by exact address
friend@gmail.com]
    C --> E[Bundle in Browse and Sweep]
    D --> E

A bundle is all the mail from one company, or from one person on a shared service like Gmail. Cleaning up a bundle handles all of it at once.

5. Reading and browsing

flowchart TD
    A[Your mail] --> B[Inbox
every account in one list]
    A --> C[Browse
Senders, Domains, Subjects,
Bundles, Messages]
    A --> D[Sweep

    A --> E[Ask Gusk

    B --> R[Open an email]
    C --> R
    R --> S{Pictures from
the web?}
    S -- Yes --> T[Blocked until you tap
Load Images]
    S -- No --> U[Email shown]
    T --> U
  • Ask Gusk runs on your device with Apple Intelligence. Your mail isn't sent to an AI cloud.
  • Pictures from the web in emails and sender logos are off by default. You can turn them on in Settings › Security.

6. Cleaning up

flowchart TD
    A[You choose an action] --> B{From where?}
    B -- Sweep --> C{Is any email in the bundle
starred or protected?}
    C -- Yes --> C1[Whole bundle left alone]
    C -- No --> D[Keep, Archive or Trash]
    B -- Selected emails --> E[Mark Read, Archive or Trash]
    B -- Ask Gusk --> F[Shows what it will do] --> G{You confirm?}
    G -- No --> H[Nothing changes]
    G -- Yes --> E
    D --> I[Action saved and logged on the device]
    E --> I
    I --> J[Sent to your mail service]
    I --> L[Undo button appears]

Protected mail: starred emails, and mail from senders or domains you protect in Settings, are never swept. If even one email in a bundle is protected, Sweep leaves the whole bundle alone.

7. Undo

flowchart TD
    A[You tap Undo] --> B{Still being sent to
your mail service?}
    B -- Yes --> C[Wait for it to finish]
    B -- No --> D
    C --> D[Work out the reverse
archived: back to the inbox
marked read: back to unread]
    D --> E[Sent to your mail service]
    E --> F[Emails back where they were]

Undo reverses only what actually changed. For example, an email that was already read before you marked it read stays read.

8. Removing an account

flowchart TD
    A[Settings, Accounts, Remove Account] --> B[Syncing stops]
    B --> C[App password deleted from the Keychain]
    C --> D[That account's mail deleted from this device]
    D --> E{Another account
signed in?}
    E -- Yes --> F[Switch to it]
    E -- No --> G[Sign-in screen
notifications and badge cleared]

Removing an account deletes its data from this device only. Your mail at your mail service isn't changed. See the privacy policy for more.
